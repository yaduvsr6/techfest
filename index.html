A Symphony of Tech, Beats, Speed & Sweat

Conceived as a platform to celebrate the unstoppable creativity of young minds, IGNITE has grown from an inter-college symposium into one of the state's largest techno-cultural spectacles.

In its seventh incarnation, IGNITE V7 shatters boundaries. Whether you are engineering autonomous drones, revving custom supercars at the Auto Expo, conquering the mat in Kabaddi, walking the high-fashion runway, or singing under 50,000 watts of festival sound at our grand Pro Night — this stage belongs to you.
